Statistical Analysis of Weights of Randomly-Selected Glass Beads

OPERATIONS

  1. The SELECT A COLOR dropdown chooses which color of bead you are weighing.
  2. The DELIVER button places randomly selected beads of that color on the balance. The number on the button is how many it delivers at once.
  3. The DUMP BEADS button removes everything on the pan, whether one bead or several. Your next delivery is a fresh random selection.
  4. The TARE button resets the balance reading to zero. The balance must be tared before every delivery.
  5. The + and − buttons change the number on the DELIVER button (from 1 to 10).

MASS MEASUREMENTS

  1. Select a color.
  2. Click TARE on the balance (the reading goes to 0.000 g).
  3. DELIVER one bead.
  4. Record the balance reading.
  5. DUMP BEADS to clear the pan.
  6. Repeat steps 2–5 until you have between 10 and 20 measurements.

THE TEST

Test H0: μ = 0.48 g against Ha: μ ≠ 0.48 g at α = 0.05, using your own sample size n. Your degrees of freedom are df = n − 1, so your critical value depends on how many beads you weighed:

Expect some disagreement. At α = 0.05, a correctly working test still rejects a true null hypothesis about 5% of the time. So roughly 1 student in 20 will reach a conclusion that disagrees with the rest of the class for the same color. That is a Type I error, not a measurement mistake — compare results across the class and count how often it happens.

Deliver 1 bead at a time. Tare, deliver, record, dump. Take 10–20 measurements per color.
